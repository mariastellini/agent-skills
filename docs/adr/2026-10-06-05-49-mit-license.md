# License the public skills under MIT

## Decision

Maria chose the standard MIT license for this repository to minimize barriers to reusing its skills. The root `LICENSE` contains the full terms and names Maria Stellini as the copyright holder for 2026.

## Alternatives

Apache 2.0 adds an explicit patent grant and more terms. GPL requires covered derivative distributions to remain under GPL. CC BY is intended for creative works and adds attribution requirements. MIT gives readers a short, familiar set of reuse terms for agent skills.

## Consequences

Readers may copy, modify, distribute, and sell the skills, including in proprietary projects. They must retain the copyright and permission notices in copies or substantial portions. The license provides no warranty.
